Kept from Serendipity

Verification stays cool — a single blue check against the warm palette, never gamified.
Accent logic
Ember — reveal progress, the commit action (NameDrop), correct answers. The brand warmth. Verify blue — identity confirmation only. Live green — presence and proximity only. Neutral CTA — charcoal in light, white in dark. Carries ordinary progression so ember stays meaningful.

03 Rules & guardrails
Do
Let the photo be the largest object on the screen at every stage. Scale the blurred layer to 1.18 so blur never shows a soft edge. Use the dark scrim + white text over imagery in both themes. Keep one primary pill per screen; everything else is neutral or ghost. Animate blur and stepper together on stage change — one gesture, one reward.
Avoid
✕ Pink/purple gradients. The warmth here is ember, and it is used sparingly. ✕ Ember on large fills — it is an accent, not a surface. ✕ Gamified trust — no XP, no confetti, no badge shelves. ✕ Partial face or eye reveal. Blur stays uniform. ✕ Neumorphic double shadows from reference 1 — borrowed the softness, not the emboss.
